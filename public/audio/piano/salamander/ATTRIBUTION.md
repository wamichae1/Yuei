# Salamander Grand Piano Samples

These MP3 files are a compact subset of the Salamander Grand Piano
recordings distributed by the Tone.js audio repository.

- Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
- Original sample author: Alexander Holm
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
- Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The subset includes only the pitches needed to keep every note generated by
Yuei.AI Note Identification within one semitone of a recorded sample.
